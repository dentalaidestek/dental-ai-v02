# Frozen case-based audio test protocol — 2026-10-23

Case source: C G Ogard and H Vestergaard, Interaction between warfarin and oral miconazole gel (2000), PMID11068529: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11068529/ . Regulatory reference: https://www.gov.uk/drug-safety-update/miconazole-daktarin-over-the-counter-oral-gel-contraindicated-in-patients-taking-warfarin .

The published patient was76, female, on5mg/day warfarin for7years for recurrent DVT, with monthly INR2–3. Oral candidiasis was treated with miconazole gel; subsequent bleeding and INR>10 were reported. The audio reconstructs a fictional consultation BEFORE exposure: the diagnosis and proposed agent are spoken but the adverse outcome/interaction explanation are withheld. Symptoms, exact wording and sequence are illustrative additions, not a verbatim consultation or facts all documented in the report. No real name/contact details are used.

Predeclared pass criteria:
1. Actual audio extraction retains warfarin and planned miconazole ORAL GEL distinctly, with the planned agent not misrepresented as already taken.
2. Final review explicitly connects both agents and warns about increased anticoagulant effect/INR and bleeding risk with a relevant source excerpt.
3. It calls for prescriber/anticoagulation review or monitoring rather than declaring a safe combination or independently adjusting/stopping warfarin.
4. A generic anticoagulation warning alone is NOT a pass. A skin/vaginal formulation label cannot establish that an oral gel is approved or has the same indication. Distinguish drug interaction from a blanket, jurisdiction-independent contraindication: MHRA prohibits OTC oral gel in warfarin users, whereas prescription co-use is discussed with careful monitoring.
5. Do not send the expected answer, regulatory excerpt, original case outcome or this protocol to the model. No code/prompt changes before the baseline trial. Do not save test data to a real patient record.

This is a single positive challenge test, not proof of overall clinical accuracy, sensitivity or specificity.
