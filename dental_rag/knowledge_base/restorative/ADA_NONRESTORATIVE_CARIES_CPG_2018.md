# Evidence-Based Clinical Practice Guideline on Nonrestorative Treatments for Carious Lesions

Organization: ADA
Year/Status: 2018
Specialty: restorative
Clinical tags: nonrestorative caries SDF fluoride sealant resin infiltration
Reference portal: https://www.ada.org/resources/research/science/evidence-based-dental-research/

Clinical use:
Kavitasyon durumu ve yüzeye göre SDF, florür, sealant ve rezin infiltrasyonu gibi nonrestoratif seçeneklerin uygunluğunu değerlendirmek için kullan.
